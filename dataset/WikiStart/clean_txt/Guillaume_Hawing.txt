# Biography
## Professional career
Before becoming a minister, he was the Director-General of the Mahatma Gandhi Private Institute in Lambanyi. He was appointed by decree on October 27, 2021, as the Minister of Pre-University Education and Literacy in the government, replacing Professor Bano Barry. On February 19, 2024, the Bernard Goumou government, of which he was a member, was dissolved by the National Committee of Reconciliation and Development (CNRD).

# Guillaume Hawing's Algorithm
In February 2016, he published an invention in mathematical algorithms that generates, organizes, and sequences prime numbers.
