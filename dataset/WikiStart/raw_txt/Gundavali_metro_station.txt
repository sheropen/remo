Gundavali is the elevated southern terminal metro station on the North-South corridor of the Red Line 7 of Mumbai Metro in Mumbai, India. This metro station was inaugurated on 20 January 2023 by Prime Minister Narendra Modi. Gundavali is connected to the Western Express Highway station on Line 1 through a 58 metre long foot overbridge.

# Station layout
## Power and signaling system
Like all other stations and railways of Mumbai metro, Gundavali station also uses 25,000 volt AC power system by overhead catenary to operate the trains.
