The 2022 Fiji Premier League, also known as the 2022 Digicel Premier League for sponsorship reasons, was the 46th season of the Fiji Premier League, the highest level of the Fijian football league system. The season began on 13 February and ended on 11 September. Rewa won their first title, while defending champions Lautoka finished second. Nasinu finished last and were relegated back to the Senior League after just one season.
