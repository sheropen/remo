The 2024 National Games of Nepal, also known as the 10th National Games of Nepal and informally Karnali 2024 are held from 17 November 2024 to 24 November 2024 in Karnali Province. The 115th board meeting of NSC during the 9th National Games held in Gandaki Province last year had announced that the 10th National Games will be held in Karnali Province from November 17 to November 24, 2024.

# Venues
Karnali Province Stadium, Surkhet
Kalinchowk Cricket Ground, Surkhet
Chaurjahari Regional Cricket Ground, West Rukum
Humla District Hockey Ground, Humla
Humla Volleyball Ground, Jumla
Chisapani Football Ground (Women's Group Stage)
Mehalkuna Football Ground (Men's Group Stage)

# Participating teams
Teams are expected from all 7 Provinces and 3 departmental Clubs of Nepal as well as a team representing Non-Resident Nepali, NRNA.

# Sports
37 disciplines(35+2 Exhibition) will be contested in the 2024 National Games.
