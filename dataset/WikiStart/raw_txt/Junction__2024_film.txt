Junction is a 2024 American thriller film written and directed by Bryan Greenberg and starring Greenberg, Ryan Eggold, Jamie Chung, Sophia Bush and Griffin Dunne. It is Greenberg's feature directorial debut. Junction was released on 26 January 2024.

# Plot
The modern-day opioid crisis in America from three different perspectives.

# Production
In May 2022, it was announced that Peck and Ho were cast in the film. Principal photography took place in Rhode Island.

# Release
In January 2024, it was announced that VMI Releasing acquired the United States distribution rights to the film, which was initially scheduled to be released in theaters and on VOD on January 24, 2024. The release date was changed to January 26, 2024.
