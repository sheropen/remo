The 2024 ISSF Junior World Cup is the annual edition of the ISSF Junior World Cup, governed by the International Shooting Sport Federation. The 10 metre air rifle and air pistol events were held in Granada, Spain in February 2024. The Shotgun events were scheduled for October 2024 in Lima, Peru.
